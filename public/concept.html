Concept build · public roadmap

A device that asks before it acts.

PIXIE is a proposed device-stewardship layer for people who want technology to show consequences, preserve provenance, and wait for consent before consequential actions.

The build path

From concept to hardware

01 · Web

The current PIXIE Holdings experience demonstrates the stewardship model in a browser.

02 · Android

Proposed launcher → LineageOS system layer → dedicated device, with the Pixel 8a as the initial reference target.

03 · Apple

File-structure conversion is under development, followed by proposed iOS/iPadOS and macOS companion surfaces.

04 · Hardware

A future purpose-built device could make consent and stewardship physical, local, and obvious.

Build discipline

  • PROPOSED — a product direction, not a shipped feature.
  • IMPLEMENTED — demonstrated in the repository and testable today.
  • UNVERIFIED — requires platform, hardware, or external-service validation.

The roadmap does not claim that PIXIE replaces iOS/iPadOS system launchers or that the dedicated hardware exists today.
