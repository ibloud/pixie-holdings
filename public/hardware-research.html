Research protocol & evidence

Identify the exact device, collect public source, map interfaces, determine the least-privileged access required, build reproducibly, validate read-only first, and preserve evidence. A source listing or exploit history does not establish a PIXIE capability.

  • Permission and ownership must be established before testing.
  • Root, bootloader unlock and flashing are not default requirements.
  • Privileged or destructive tests require a separate safety/recovery review and explicit approval.
  • Every capability carries source, device/version, test date, result, limitations and confidence.
  • Unknown or untested capability remains marked unverified.
