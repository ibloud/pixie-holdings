Creator production pipeline

      
The DAW is a source—not a disposable upload step

      
PIXIE preserves the Loptr Lab DAW session, source recordings, stems, mixes, masters, captions, artwork, credits, and release manifests as related but distinct assets.

      

        
Create
Loptr Lab DAW, video editor, writing, design, task, and production tools keep their editable originals.

        
Review
PIXIE previews filenames, rights, versions, captions, destinations, and rollback before handoff.

        
Play
A standards-based HTML5 audio/video package works natively and can be enhanced by Plyr with WebVTT captions.

        
Share
An approved master receives an Audio.com metadata handoff; upload and Public/Unlisted visibility remain creator decisions.

      

      
Aether OS is a possible browser host to test later. No native Aether integration, security guarantee, or partnership is claimed. Audio.com upload is manual or requires a future documented/approved API.
