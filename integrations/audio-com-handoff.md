# Audio.com handoff

PIXIE prepares metadata; the creator performs or separately authorizes the upload.

1. Export the approved mix or spoken-word master from the DAW.
2. Review title, description, tags, license, and visibility in the production manifest.
3. Upload through Audio.com's supported interface.
4. Choose `Unlisted` for review or `Public` for discovery.
5. Copy the resulting track URL or official embed code into the source record.
6. Keep the DAW session, stems, and private notes outside the public embed.

Audio.com documents manual upload and embed workflows. This prototype does not
claim or simulate an undocumented upload API.
