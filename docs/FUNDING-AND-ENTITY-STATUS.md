# Loptr Lab organizational support and financial-status record

**Status date:** 22 November 2026  
**Purpose:** distinguish the support routes people can use from legal-entity status, accounting records, and project-specific commercial agreements.

This is an evidence and operating-boundary document, not a financial statement, tax return, audit, legal opinion, charitable solicitation registration, or confirmation of any entity's formation.

## Current support routes

Loptr Lab accepts optional organizational support through these owner-designated destinations:

- [Buy Me a Coffee](https://buymeacoffee.com/loptrlab)
- [Patreon](https://www.patreon.com/LoptrLab/)
- [GitHub Sponsors](https://github.com/sponsors/ibloud)
- [PayPal.me/ibloud](https://paypal.me/ibloud)

Each platform controls its own available payment options, tier names, amounts, fees, terms, and any displayed benefits. The current platform configuration is the source for those details; this document does not invent a unified price list or promise platform benefits that have not been checked.

These are ways to support the organization, not four membership gates or a Mayhem progression ladder. Supporting financially is optional. A person's ability to play, learn, contribute, collaborate, be heard, receive appropriate credit, or propose work does not depend on payment. Payment does not buy rank, contributor status, governance votes, canon authority, private access, approvals, employment, ownership, or rights in project IP.

## What support is intended to sustain

Support is intended to help sustain shared infrastructure, maintenance, accessibility work, and documented creative and technical development. It is organizational support, not a purchase of a particular project's intellectual property or a promise that a specific project, stipend, job, event, product, or feature will be funded.

No organization-wide percentage allocation, restricted-fund policy, approved operating budget, or audited allocation is established by this record. Any future paid commission, stipend, internship, teaching engagement, commercial license, royalty share, or B2B production requires an identified contracting party, a scoped budget, rights and tax review, and written terms before commitment.

## Current legal and financial evidence boundary

The public repository materials reviewed for this update do **not** establish:

- a selected or formed umbrella business entity, holding company, subsidiary, nonprofit, or tax-exempt organization;
- the legal account holder and ultimate recipient for each payment platform;
- an EIN, tax election, tax-exempt determination, or verified filing status;
- consolidated platform receipts, gross-to-net payout reconciliations, refunds, chargebacks, fees, bank deposits, or expense ledgers;
- entity-level financial statements, audited accounts, tax returns, information returns, or a verified tax-reporting package.

This is a statement about the public materials reviewed, not a conclusion that no entity, private bookkeeping, bank records, tax filings, or other private records exist. No private platform dashboards, payout accounts, bank records, contracts, or tax documents were inspected, and no official Minnesota entity registry search was completed for this update.

The labels “Loptr Lab,” “PIXIE Holdings,” “Gods of Mayhem,” and repository/domain names do not identify the legal payee or establish a legal entity. Do not describe these payments as charitable or tax-deductible, or issue an entity invoice or tax receipt, unless the actual recipient, legal status, and applicable requirements have been verified.

## Reporting readiness and next evidence

Before presenting consolidated financials or making entity-specific tax claims, reconcile each support channel separately:

1. Confirm the account owner, legal payee, payout destination, and tax profile shown by the provider.
2. Preserve provider statements showing gross receipts, dates, fees, refunds, chargebacks, net payouts, and any tax forms.
3. Match net payouts to the actual receiving bank or payment account and record the recipient.
4. Retain invoices and source receipts for expenses; classify business, personal, restricted, and project-specific funds correctly.
5. Reconcile records by tax year and obtain qualified tax/accounting advice on the actual recipient and business structure before filing or making public claims.

The IRS explains that tax reporting depends on the actual business structure. For example, a sole proprietorship has no legal identity separate from its individual owner; this document does not determine that Loptr Lab or any named project is a sole proprietorship. See [IRS Topic 407: Business income](https://www.irs.gov/taxtopics/tc407), [IRS Publication 583: Starting a Business and Keeping Records](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p583), and [IRS recordkeeping guidance](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/what-kind-of-records-should-i-keep).

## Relationship to Mayhem and commercial partnerships

Gods of Mayhem remains an open participation and competitive/community pathway. Its free play, low-cost materials, reuse, workshop, and contribution options are participation choices—not sponsorship levels. Optional support links sustain Loptr Lab's work and do not determine who belongs.

B2B game partnerships, licensed editions, commissions, distribution, and royalty arrangements are separate from platform support. They require project-specific rights clearance, an identified contracting party, negotiated deliverables and compensation, accessibility expectations, and signed terms. A sponsorship payment alone grants no commercial, trademark, artwork, distribution, derivative-work, or ownership rights.
