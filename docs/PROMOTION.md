# Promotion path

## Core line

**Alexa+ is the voice. AT Protocol is the portable body. Obsidian is the creator-controlled memory. PIXIE makes the system ask before it acts.**

## 30 seconds

PIXIE Holdings is an accessibility-first economics game from Loptr Lab. You manage three creative projects with limited money and human capacity. Alexa+ explains the choices, PIXIE previews the consequences, and an optional AT Protocol receipt makes approved decisions portable without exposing the creator's private vault. You can remain solvent—but only if you account for access, rights, trust, and the people doing the work.

## 90 seconds

Most business simulations reward growth and hide what gets externalized. PIXIE Holdings makes those costs playable. The portfolio includes Veiled Dominion, Break the Grid, and Float Works. Every allocation changes six ledgers: cash, human capacity, accessibility, rights, trust, and system pressure. The assistant cannot spend, publish, or infer health. It explains tradeoffs and waits for approval. Alexa+ supplies the voice interface, AT Protocol supplies a portable public evidence layer, and Narrative Provenance in Obsidian remains the private source of truth. The project turns Dominique Devereaux's lived accessibility experience into systems architecture rather than treating disability as a marketing story.

## Three-minute demonstration

1. Open with the question: “Can your portfolio grow without making people pay the hidden cost?”
2. Show the six ledgers and three holdings.
3. Ask Alexa+/PIXIE for the current portfolio briefing.
4. Select “Ship now” for one holding and review its access, trust, and pressure consequences.
5. Cancel to demonstrate agency and reversibility.
6. Select an accessibility or community investment and approve it.
7. Show the updated ledgers and public-receipt preview.
8. Close on the architecture: voice, body, memory, steward.

## Promotion sequence

### Before a working deployment

- Share the problem and architectural question, not claims of completed functionality.
- Link to the existing PIXIE evidence and Loptr Lab ecosystem.
- Label screenshots “development prototype” and all figures “synthetic.”

### After the repository is public

- Publish a build thread: problem → architecture → working decision → accessibility boundary.
- Tag platforms only when the post directly concerns their documented technology.
- Invite accessibility testers, economic-game designers, AT Protocol developers, and Alexa+ reviewers.
- Use one canonical link: the prototype landing page.

### At submission

- Lead the video with the working MCP interaction.
- State exactly what was built during the hackathon.
- Publish the friction log and product feedback required by Devpost.
- Never imply endorsement by Amazon, Bluesky, Obsidian, artists, the Financial Times, or third-party franchises.

## Calls to action

- “Test one decision with a screen reader.”
- “Review the synthetic economic model.”
- “Help define a public consent-receipt Lexicon.”
- “Show us which cost ordinary business dashboards hide.”

## Promotion gates

Do not announce a live Alexa+ integration until the MCP endpoint is deployed and demonstrated. Do not announce AT publication until OAuth, preview, revocation language, and deletion limitations are tested. Do not use personal health details beyond Dominique's chosen public founder statement.
