# Product requirements

## Primary user

A creator, producer, or founder who needs to make economic decisions while accounting for limited money, energy, access, rights, and community responsibility.

## Core journey

1. Enter the fictional portfolio and receive a clear explanation.
2. Review three holdings and the available quarterly budget.
3. Select one proposed allocation.
4. Read PIXIE's preview of financial and human consequences.
5. Approve or cancel without penalty.
6. Review updated ledgers and an explanation of what the decision externalized.
7. Preview a minimal public provenance receipt or export the session privately.

## Requirements

- Keyboard and screen-reader operable
- Visible focus, semantic headings, live status announcements
- Reduced-motion support and no flashing presentation
- Plain-language consequence summaries
- No decision executes before explicit approval
- No punitive streaks, hidden scoring, diagnosis, or behavioral inference
- Game state remains local by default
- All scenario figures visibly labeled synthetic
- Third-party references remain outside the playable product

## Promotion requirements

- Explain how the prototype connects to PIXIE, Veiled Dominion, Break the Grid, Duet, Made Sick, Narrative Provenance, and AT Protocol
- Provide 30-second, 90-second, and three-minute descriptions
- State what was created during the hackathon
- Invite specific technical, accessibility, research, and economic-design contributions
