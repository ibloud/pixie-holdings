# Scope

## One-sentence promise

Use Alexa+ and a browser-based PIXIE interface to steward a fictional creative portfolio without sacrificing people, accessibility, rights, or creator control for growth.

## In scope

- Three holdings: Veiled Dominion, Break the Grid, and Float Works
- One-quarter allocation loop with synthetic budgets
- Six ledgers: cash, capacity, access, rights, trust, and pressure
- Accessible decision interface and consequence explanation
- Local save, restart, and JSON export
- Self-hosted MCP endpoint using protocol version 2025-11-25
- AT Protocol-compatible public-receipt preview
- Promotion plan connecting the prototype to the Loptr Lab ecosystem

## Out of scope

- Real investments, payments, orders, valuations, or securities
- Private health records, therapist coordination, health inference, food/location monitoring
- Automatic Obsidian access or publication
- Third-party characters, music, likenesses, trademarks, or implied partnerships
- Full AT OAuth, production PDS writes, multiplayer, and production hosting

## Success

A judge can complete one decision, hear or read its tradeoffs, see all six ledgers update, understand the accessibility thesis, inspect real MCP code, and see a consent-gated AT record preview in under three minutes.
