# Ecosystem relationship

PIXIE Holdings is a demonstration layer, not a replacement for the projects it references.

| Project | Role in the demonstration | Authority boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Loptr Lab | Studio and public ecosystem entrance | Own identity; not a fictional third-party corporation |
| Veiled Dominion | Original governance and restraint holding | Canon remains in its own repository |
| Break the Grid | System-pressure and community-investment holding | Independent Loptr Lab prototype; no artist endorsement implied |
| Duet | Accessibility and low-demand interaction reference | Experimental mechanics do not become Veiled Dominion canon automatically |
| PIXIE Device Stewardship | Consent, preview, reversibility, and continuity principles | Not a clinician, financial adviser, or autonomous operator |
| Narrative Provenance | Private evidence and rights memory | Local-only plugin; no automatic network access |
| Made Sick | Voluntary creator-care and economic-agency pathway | Health information is not required or public by default |
| AT Protocol | Portable public identity and approved evidence | Carries records; does not adjudicate ownership, legality, or consent |

The playable company and holdings are original satirical constructs. References to fictional corporations or well-known AI antagonists explain the genre; they are not part of the product.
