# Refurbished Network Equipment & DIY Hosting — Partnership Log

**Status:** Research and partner discovery; no partner, quote, purchase, or endorsement is established.  
**Review date:** 23 November 2026  
**Audience:** DIYers, homelab builders, independent creators, small hosting operators, accessibility-minded learners, and community infrastructure projects.

## Why this belongs in Holdings

The ecosystem needs a practical path to ownership without assuming “self-host” means buying new enterprise gear. Refurbished network and server hardware can lower entry cost, extend useful life, and let people learn on real equipment. The lowest sticker price is not necessarily the lowest total cost: power, cooling, noise, licensing, firmware support, optics, missing rails/PSUs, shipping, warranty, and recovery time all matter.

Our target is not enterprise procurement alone. Investigate a **DIYer-friendly pathway**: transparent parts lists, tested equipment, clear compatibility, accessible documentation, small starter bundles, and optional support—not lock-in, sponsorship obligations, or an upsell funnel.

## First cost observations (not quotes)

Public listings reviewed 23 November 2026 show wide variation by age, model, condition, warranty, and license status. A certified-used marketplace displayed examples including older managed switches roughly **$20–$80**, some PoE switches around **$50–$180**, and higher-capability gear ranging into hundreds. These are listing snapshots, not recommended models or price guarantees.

Evaluate three tiers, not a fixed shopping list:
- **Entry / learning network:** low-power router/firewall, manageable switch (PoE only if needed), access point, cables, and small host. Avoid mandatory cloud subscriptions or unsupported firmware.
- **Creator / small service node:** modest refurbished server or mini-PC, UPS, network segmentation, storage/backup, and monitoring.
- **Lab / multi-service rack:** refurbished enterprise server, managed PoE switching, rack/UPS, redundant storage/network components. Higher purchase price, power, and noise may be unsuitable for a home/studio.

For any exact model, record hardware revision, firmware/support status, license/activation requirements, power at idle/load, fan/noise, warranty/return window, tested condition, seller, price, shipping, and date. Avoid Meraki or similar license-dependent devices unless ownership release and ongoing requirements are verified.

## DIYer-centered evaluation checklist

- Exact model and hardware revision; no vague “enterprise switch” descriptions.
- Tested and reset; firmware/source and support status documented.
- License/activation/cloud-controller dependency disclosed and verified.
- Power draw and estimated monthly electricity cost included.
- Fan/noise, heat, mounting, cable/optic compatibility, and dimensions documented.
- Warranty, return policy, shipping, missing parts, and seller support explicit.
- Accessible instructions; no jargon-only setup.
- No required account or telemetry beyond necessary functionality.
- No forced upsell, exclusivity, affiliate tracking, or data-sharing arrangement.
- Compare refurbished enterprise gear with low-power mini-PC, prosumer network kit, VPS, and managed service.
