# PIXIE Legal & Rights — project intake framework (review draft)

**Status:** proposed governance and intake wording, **not legal advice, an approved contract, a rights clearance decision or an automated legal assessment**. Owner placement decision: Holdings maintains the baseline, Companion applies questions to a specific brief, Creator OS retains the existing asset-level review and sharing hold. Independent legal research and professional review remain pending. Do not call this framework legally validated.

## Baseline questions — every project

Use only information the creator chooses to provide. A project can be saved as a private draft without answering all questions; missing answers are **unknown**, not consent or clearance.

1. **Project and purpose:** title, brief, intended audience, educational/private/public/commercial use, jurisdiction(s) if known, intended distribution platforms and dates.
2. **Participants and scope:** people or organizations involved, their roles, whether any are minors, contact route controlled by the participant, tasks, deliverables, milestones, approval and exit path.
3. **Contributions and rights:** who created each composition, master, code, image, lyric, performance, recording and other input; source links and original files; ownership **claims** versus verified documentation; third-party licenses and restrictions.
4. **Permissions and restrictions:** sample/interpolation/cover and sync uses, likeness/voice/performer permissions, platform terms, use of AI-generated or AI-assisted content, and any open questions.
5. **Money and credit:** paid, unpaid, royalty, split or sponsorship *proposal*; credit/attribution preferences; reimbursement, cancellations and refunds where relevant. Do not infer employment, work-made-for-hire, transfer or revenue share.
6. **Data and accessibility:** what private or sensitive information is actually necessary, retention/deletion expectations, intended collaborators, consent to any upload or public disclosure, accessible communication and participation needs without requesting disability diagnosis.
7. **Review and sign-off:** who may approve edits, licensing, publication and payments; versioned decisions, evidence, unresolved risks and referral to a qualified professional.

## Conditional addenda — based on the brief

| Trigger | Additional review or draft (not automatically binding) |
| --- | --- |
| Music release, producer collaboration, sample or cover | Composition/master ownership, splits, credits, producer services, sample/publisher/recording permissions, distribution and mechanical/sync questions |
| Suno or other AI music, voice cloning, generated imagery | Provider terms at the relevant generation/use date, inputs and outputs, human contribution, voice/likeness permissions, provenance and unresolved copyright/contract issues |
| Video, performance, photographs or identifiable voices | Performer/location releases, recording consent, publicity and distribution rights, soundtrack clearance |
| Live event, stream, ticket or sponsorship | Host/platform terms, venue and territory, performer and stream rights, accessibility, moderation, admission, capacity, refunds, consumer disclosures, payment provider and tax questions |
| Children or youth participants | Age-appropriate guardian authorization, child privacy and labor/performer considerations, no public disclosure by default |
| Employment, paid contractor, volunteer or revenue share | Classification, compensation, tax, IP assignment/licensing, benefits and applicable local labor rules; qualified review before binding terms |
| Cross-border collaboration or sensitive personal data | Applicable law, transfers, privacy notices, retention and specialized professional review |

## Rules for PIXIE presentation

- Show **baseline prompts** and conditional **review flags**, never a generated “legally cleared” verdict.
- **Unknown / needs evidence / needs professional review / documented permission** are descriptive statuses, not legal conclusions. The presence of a file or checkbox alone is not proof of a valid license.
- Keep an existing Creator OS clearance-dependent external sharing hold in place; a Holdings page or Companion intake answer must never bypass it.
- Do not automatically send confidential briefs, correspondence, contracts, files, identifiers or personal details to a model, GitHub issue, public URL or third-party platform.
- Distinguish voluntary community review from paid professional engagement; an intake response does not create a contract, employment, agency, artist endorsement or representation.
- Generate only clearly labeled **draft questions and draft agreement outlines** for human review. No automatic signatures, legal advice, payment, public publishing or binding acceptance.
- Review wording against the forthcoming Deep Research report and jurisdiction-specific counsel before enabling real-world legal templates or claims of compliance.
