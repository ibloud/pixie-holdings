## Owner direction — 28 October 2026

Holdings is PIXIE’s business-facing, command-led review surface for people inspecting the results of Creator OS work on the Atmosphere. Made-Sick is the existing community/join destination. This direction does not establish connected report ingestion, an installed CLI, shared accounts or automatic publication.

- Reuse current work; inspect open PRs and unpublished changes first. Privacy correction PR #60 remains a separate assignment. Do not merge it into design work or restore URL-driven participant onboarding.
- One authoritative stylesheet: public/styles.css. Tokens and shared components precede narrow, justified surface exceptions. No HTML style blocks, injected style blocks, copied themes or static style attributes. Runtime window position, visibility and canvas geometry may remain JS-controlled; they must not define typography, colors or component themes.
- Adapt Material 3 roles and accessibility principles to the approved PIXIE palette. Normal heading tracking, readable line height, paired text/surface contrast, balanced gutters, semantic labels and visible keyboard focus take priority over visual effects.
- Onboarding demonstrates one useful result, its evidence and an explicit next action. Disclose sample data before interaction. Keep source, rights, permission and uncertainty attached. Do not substitute market quotes for creative/business outcomes.
- Preserve the AetherOS desktop/command reference as design lineage supplied by Dominique; do not claim affiliation, shared runtime or assets/license rights.
- No identity, consent flag or private report in destination URLs. Made-Sick owns its authenticated join and consent flow. Do not modify Made-Sick, Duet, 50 Ways, Training or other products under a Holdings design assignment.
- Record design changes and proposed versus implemented behavior in docs/BRAND_GUIDELINES.md. Prefer existing canonical guidelines in future; do not create competing sources.
- Produce a Vercel PREVIEW and verify it before requesting owner review. Do not push to main, merge, promote or change production/domain configuration without explicit owner authorization for that release.
- Check desktop and narrow widths, command keyboard/button paths, invalid input, links and existing tests. State physical iPad/Safari/VoiceOver and live-service limits honestly.
